Questions, answered

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. Both platforms support secure access permissions — we set it up with you at onboarding without you sharing a password.

Who writes the posts?

Our content team builds everything from your brand kit and voice intake, and a human reviews every post before it publishes. You'll recognize your business in every post — that's the standard.

Can I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. Tell us at intake and we'll run on a client-approval flow: you get the content calendar up front and nothing posts without your sign-off.

What about video?

We post videos you supply. We never generate AI video — your brand deserves better than that, so we simply don't make it.

What results should I expect?

A consistently active, professional presence — daily posts, answered DMs and comments, and (on Pro and Elite) reporting that shows you exactly what happened each month. We won't promise you fake follower numbers; nobody honest can.

What if I want to cancel?

Month to month, cancel anytime from your billing portal. No exit fees, no retention phone calls — we don't do phone calls at all.

What if I need help?

Jump on our live chat at almadigitalservices.com — Alex from our team is available 24/7 and can help you instantly!
