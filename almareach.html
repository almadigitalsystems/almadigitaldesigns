Questions, answered

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It's trained on your business and your tone, and it's tested in real conversations before launch. Customers ask real questions and get real answers — in full sentences, in their language.

What if the AI doesn't know the answer?

It says so honestly and hands the conversation to you or your team immediately, with full context. It never guesses about your prices or policies.

Do I need a new phone or app?

No. Your assistant runs on a dedicated US WhatsApp number we provision and manage. Your existing phone and number stay exactly as they are.

What do you need from me?

About 20 minutes: your hours, services, prices, common questions, and how you want it to sound. Send it as a list, a document, or photos of your price sheet — we'll do the rest.

How do I update it later?

Email us the change — new prices, new hours, new products — and we update your assistant. It's included.

What if I need help?

Real human support by email — first response within 1 business day. If your assistant ever goes down, we respond within 4 hours, any day of the week.
