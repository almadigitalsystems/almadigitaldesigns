The Problem

Serra Industries has been a trusted name in Miami's live event production scene for over 22 years, delivering professional audio, lighting, video, and staging for everything from corporate conferences to large-scale concerts.

But despite decades of hands-on expertise and 500+ successful events, their online presence didn't match the caliber of their work. Without a professional website, potential clients searching for event production services in Miami had no way to evaluate Serra Industries' capabilities, view past work, or request a quote — costing them leads at the exact moment prospects were ready to hire.

The Solution

Alma Digital Designs built Serra Industries a clean, modern, mobile-friendly website at serraindustries.com that showcases their full range of services and establishes immediate credibility.

  • Professional design with a confidence-building layout, including credibility metrics (22+ years experience, 500+ events, 100% client satisfaction)
  • Clear service breakdown across all eight core competencies — from audio engineering and intelligent lighting to LED walls and custom staging
  • Portfolio section showcasing past event work
  • Multiple lead capture paths including prominent "Get a Quote" CTAs and a dedicated contact page
  • Client testimonials with 5-star ratings to build social proof
  • SSL-secured with custom domain, hosted on reliable infrastructure
  • Mobile-optimized so prospects on any device get a professional experience

The Result

Serra Industries now has a professional digital storefront that matches the quality of their 22+ years of live event production work. The site gives prospects a clear picture of capabilities, past work, and an easy path to request a quote — turning online searches into real business conversations.
